### Federated search: every source has its own outcome, and the answer comes within the 5 s deadline
$ curl -s -o /tmp/fed-demo-search.json -w 'HTTP %{http_code} in %{time_total} s\n' 'http://localhost:8080/apis/registry/v3/search/federated?skill=translate'

$ jq -c '.sources[]' /tmp/fed-demo-search.json

{"source":"down","outcome":"failed","reason":"unreachable","count":0,"truncated":false}
{"source":"legacy","outcome":"unsupported","count":0,"truncated":false}
{"source":"remote","outcome":"ok","count":1,"truncated":false}
{"source":"remote-credentialed","outcome":"ok","count":1,"truncated":false}
{"source":"remote-missing-key","outcome":"failed","reason":"credential_unavailable","count":0,"truncated":false}

### A search that another registry forwarded is refused, so a search goes at most one hop
$ curl -s -H 'X-Federation-Hop: 1' 'http://localhost:8080/apis/registry/v3/search/federated' | jq -c '{status, title}'
{"status":400,"title":"A federated search cannot be forwarded: the request carries the X-Federation-Hop header."}


### perSourceLimit below 1 is refused
$ curl -s 'http://localhost:8080/apis/registry/v3/search/federated?perSourceLimit=0' | jq -c '{status, title}'
{"status":400,"title":"perSourceLimit must be at least 1."}
